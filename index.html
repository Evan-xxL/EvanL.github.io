AI in my work

An agent that writes the meeting recap for you

Problem
After customer meetings, recaps went out late or not at all. Writing them up was tedious, so it slipped.
What I built
An agent in Realm that takes the Chorus transcript, cross-references internal notes in Slack and earlier email threads, and drafts a recap with decisions, action items and owners. Then it prompts the meeting owner to review and send.
Outcome
My team adopted it right away. It removed a step people already disliked and didn't add a new one.
