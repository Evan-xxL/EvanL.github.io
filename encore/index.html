    How the data gets cleaned

    Spotify exports come in two formats with different field names, and files often overlap. Encore detects the format, removes exact duplicate rows, sets aside podcasts and rows with no track, and counts plays under 30 seconds as skips rather than listens, which matches the threshold Spotify uses for a stream. Artist photos and album covers are looked up by name from Deezer's public catalog, and anything not found gets a generated cover.

    Get your own data

    1. Sign in at spotify.com, open your account, and go to Privacy settings.
    2. Request Account data (faster) or Extended streaming history (more detail).
    3. Spotify emails you a download link. Drop the .zip here.
