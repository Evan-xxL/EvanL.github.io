Market Pulse -

A scheduled job pulls US market quotes and news from the Finnhub API after every close, tags the headlines, refreshes this page, and posts a brief to Discord or Slack through a webhook. No servers to run.

    How it works GitHub Actions + Finnhub + webhooks

    01 · ScheduleGitHub ActionsRuns every weekday after the 4 pm close, or on demand.
    02 · APIFinnhub REST APIAbout 25 quote calls plus the general news feed.
    03 · TransformPythonCleans the data, tags headline tone and topics, writes the brief.
    04 · PublishThis pageNew JSON is committed and GitHub Pages rebuilds.
    05 · NotifyWebhooksPosts the brief to Discord and Slack channels.
    curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer <token>" \ https://api.github.com/repos/<owner>/<repo>/dispatches \ -d '{"event_type":"market-brief"}' # trigger a fresh brief from any app
